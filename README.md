# Mancala
Play it here: https://kriishnaa-18.github.io/Mancala/

A Mancala game you can play against the computer, built with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. Based on a C version I wrote for CS 221.

## How to play
- Tap one of your pits (bottom row) to sow its seeds around the board.
- Land your last seed in your store to go again.
- Land it in an empty pit on your side to capture the seeds across from it.
- The game ends when one side is empty. Most seeds wins.
