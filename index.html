Mancala

Think you can outsmart the bot? It definitely thinks you can't. Tap a pit on the bottom row and prove it wrong.

How to play
  • Your turf: The bottom row is yours. Tap a pit and its seeds get dropped one by one, moving right, into your store and then onto the bot's side.
  • Hands off the bot's store: Your seeds skip it. Rude, but those are the rules.
  • Free turn: If your last seed lands in your own store, you go again. The bot hates this.
  • Steal mode: If your last seed lands in an empty pit on your side, you grab everything in the pit across from it, plus your own seed. Villain arc unlocked.
  • Game over: When one side runs dry, the leftover seeds go to whoever owns that side. Most seeds wins, and the bot will not be gracious about it.
  • Bragging rights: Your best score is saved, so beat your own record.

Mancala

Think you can outsmart the bot? It definitely thinks you can't. Tap a pit on the bottom row and prove it wrong.

How to play
  • Pick one of your pits (bottom row). Its seeds are dropped one by one, moving right, into your store, then around the other side.
  • Your seeds skip the opponent's store.
  • If your last seed lands in your own store, you go again.
  • If your last seed lands in an empty pit on your side and the pit across has seeds, you capture both.
  • The game ends when a side is empty. Remaining seeds go to their owner's store. Most seeds wins.
