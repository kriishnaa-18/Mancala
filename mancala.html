Mancala

Play against a computer opponent. You sow along the bottom row; tap a pit to start.

How to play
  • Pick one of your pits (bottom row). Its seeds are dropped one by one, moving right, into your store, then around the other side.
  • Your seeds skip the opponent's store.
  • If your last seed lands in your own store, you go again.
  • If your last seed lands in an empty pit on your side and the pit across has seeds, you capture both.
  • The game ends when a side is empty. Remaining seeds go to their owner's store. Most seeds wins.
